01 — INTRODUCTION

Why FreshSense?

Motivation

Food waste is a major global issue. Consumers often discard food because they cannot accurately estimate its freshness or remaining shelf life.

Traditional methods mainly rely on expiration dates and manual observation. However, food deterioration also depends on temperature, humidity and storage conditions.

FreshSense aims to address this problem through an AI-powered platform that combines Computer Vision and Machine Learning to monitor food freshness and predict potential spoilage.

Project Objectives

  • Recognize food products from images.
  • Estimate visible freshness levels.
  • Predict the remaining shelf life.
  • Recommend which products should be consumed first.
  • Suggest recipes using available ingredients.
  • Help reduce unnecessary household food waste.

Expected Outcome

We aim to develop an intelligent web application that monitors food inventory, provides freshness estimates, predicts shelf life and offers personalized recommendations.

02 — DATASETS

Understanding the Data

The project will use food image datasets and structured information related to food characteristics and storage conditions.

Potential Data Sources

  • Fresh and Rotten Fruits Dataset.
  • Fruits and Vegetables Image Dataset.
  • Temperature and humidity measurements.
  • Food purchase dates and storage duration.

Data Preprocessing

Images will be resized and normalized. Structured data will be cleaned, encoded and prepared for model training. The dataset will be divided into training, validation and testing subsets.

03 — METHODOLOGY

Our AI Approach

1. Food Recognition

CNN-based models will identify food categories from uploaded images. MobileNetV2 and EfficientNet will be considered.

2. Freshness Classification

Image classification models will estimate visible freshness categories, such as fresh and spoiled.

3. Shelf-Life Prediction

Regression algorithms such as Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and XGBoost will be investigated to estimate remaining shelf life using available food and storage features.

4. Smart Recommendations

The system will prioritize products according to their estimated remaining shelf life and suggest recipes based on available ingredients.
